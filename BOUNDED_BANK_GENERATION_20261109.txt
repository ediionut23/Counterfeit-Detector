Allocation
Before1M, deterministic allocations target25% coverage work,15% exploration,
60% volume yield. Method floors use job counts, not millisecond CPU ratios.
Yield scores include estimated cooling wall cost. Category coverage remains
distinct named molecule/category membership, separately from globally new
molecules. Once volume is complete, remaining original category floors still
must be met; no count or taxonomy is invented to satisfy a floor.

At most25% of M jobs explicitly target a fine subtype. The remaining jobs search
the same actual broad operator category, allowing mixed subtypes across depths.
Known zero-yield reference/category/subtype pairs are deferred after2 complete
attempts. Observed feasibility and logarithmically scaled coverage replace raw
legacy fine-count domination. Produced subcategories/history remain those of the
unchanged engine, never a requested label assigned after generation.

Cooling
The single native compute child and90s hard deadline are unchanged. Short-job,
coordinator, pressure-probe and status-write wall time accumulates toward15s
nominal /5s fair-or-unknown active budgets. Cooling debt is3x /11x work; minimum
interjob rest is0.05s. Serious/critical pressure requires120s sustained nominal.
Native chemistry is never SIGSTOPped. A single native job can overrun the budget
up to its90s deadline; its full cooling debt must be repaid before any next job.
Thus this is a conservative long-run duty policy, not a guarantee that every
15s sliding window or the case temperature stays below a measured threshold.
